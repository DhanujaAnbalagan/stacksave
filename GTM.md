# Go-To-Market (GTM) Plan — StackSave

## 1. Target Persona
**The Exact User:** Fractional CFOs and "Player-Coach" VPs of Engineering at Series A startups (20–60 employees).

**Why them?**
- **Fractional CFOs**: They manage 3–5 startups at once. They are incentivized to find "quick wins" to prove their value. AI spend is currently their biggest "black box."
- **VPs of Engineering**: They hate administrative billing work but are under pressure to reduce "Cloud & SaaS" burn. They want a tool that gives them the data to force their team to consolidate.

## 2. The "Pre-Intent" Moment
What are they doing right before they need StackSave?
- They are in a **monthly budget review meeting** where the "Software" line item just jumped 25%.
- They are scrolling **Hacker News** or **r/SaaS** looking for "OpenAI API cost optimization" after a surprise $2,000 bill.
- They are evaluating a switch from **GitHub Copilot to Cursor** and want to know if it's worth the price jump.

## 3. Distribution Channels ($0 Budget)

### A. The "Fractional CFO" Network
- **Action**: Reach out to the top 10 Fractional CFO agencies (e.g., Kruze Consulting, Burkland) and offer them a "Private Label" version of the audit tool for their clients.
- **Why**: One CFO can bring 20+ startup audits. It’s a high-leverage referral loop.

### B. "Model-Comparison" Community Engineering
- **Action**: Create a "Real-World Cost Comparison" post on r/startups and r/v0dev showing the delta between ChatGPT Team and Plus for small teams. 
- **Hook**: "I built a tool that found $4k in wasted AI spend at my last startup. Here's the data."

### C. The "Twitter/X Audit" Bot
- **Action**: A simple script that monitors keywords like "AI bill too high" or "Claude vs ChatGPT price" and replies with: *"Just ran into this. I used StackSave to check our team's stack—found we were overspending by $300/mo. Here's the link if you want to check yours."*

## 4. The Unfair Advantage (Credex Synergy)
StackSave is the **Trojan Horse for Credex**. 
- Most optimization tools just give advice. StackSave gives advice + **the immediate execution path** (Credex credits). 
- We don't just say "Switch to Claude"; we say "Switch to Claude and get it 20% cheaper through Credex." This makes the ROI of using the tool 100% higher than any competitor.
