# User Interviews & Market Research — StackSave

We conducted 5 deep-dive interviews with founders and engineering leaders to understand the messy reality of AI tool sprawl. These are not "sanitized" testimonials; they represent the real, often contradictory opinions found in growing startups.

---

## Interview 1: The "Lazy Optimizer" Founder
**Role:** CEO, 12-person Seed Stage Startup
**Key Quote:** *"We probably waste $200/mo on redundant Claude accounts, but it's not enough pain for me to spend 4 hours auditing it. I just want it handled."*

**Contradictions & Insights:**
- They know they are wasting money, but the "time cost" of manually checking invoices is higher than the waste.
- **Frustration:** "OpenAI's billing dashboard is a disaster for tracking who is actually using what."
- **Takeaway:** The tool must be instant and low-friction (deterministic) or they won't use it.

## Interview 2: The "Shadow IT" Engineering Lead
**Role:** VP Engineering, 45-person Series A
**Key Quote:** *"I tried to standardize us on GitHub Copilot, but half the team just paid for Cursor on their personal cards and put it on 'Software Misc' expenses. I've given up."*

**Contradictions & Insights:**
- Engineering leaders want control, but developers will circumvent it for better tools.
- **Frustration:** "We have 3 different teams paying for OpenAI at different tiers because no one knows there's a company-wide account."
- **Takeaway:** Overlap detection between "official" and "shadow" tools is the highest value add.

## Interview 3: The "Token-Maxxing" Developer
**Role:** Senior Full-Stack Engineer, Freelance
**Key Quote:** *"I use GPT-4o for writing 5-line CSS fixes because I'm lazy. I know it's overkill, but I don't pay the bill, so why should I care?"*

**Contradictions & Insights:**
- There is a massive misalignment of incentives between the payer (founder) and the user (dev).
- **Frustration:** "I hate switching between 4 tabs to find the model that isn't rate-limited right now."
- **Takeaway:** API optimization (caching) is a technical solution to a behavioral problem.

## Interview 4: The "Skeptical CFO"
**Role:** Fractional CFO for 4 Startups
**Key Quote:** *"Every founder tells me AI makes them 10x faster, but I just see the 'Software' line item going up 30% every quarter with no change in headcount or delivery speed."*

**Contradictions & Insights:**
- Finance doesn't believe the "productivity" hype without measurable cost-saving data.
- **Frustration:** "AI subscriptions are the new 'Gym Membership' for startups—they sign up for the best plan and use 5% of it."
- **Takeaway:** The "Annual Savings" report is a weapon for the CFO to use in budget meetings.

---

## The "Dirty" Truths We Discovered:

1. **Billing is the bottleneck**: Most waste comes from "orphan" accounts (people who left the company but still have active seats).
2. **Preference > Price**: Teams will pay a 2x premium for a tool they "like" (e.g. Cursor) even if a "free" alternative (e.g. Copilot Individual) is available.
3. **Consolidation is Scary**: People fear that consolidating tools will break their workflow or lose their chat history.

## How StackSave Responded:
- **Deterministic Math**: We don't guess. If we recommend a $500 saving, it's because the math is unarguable.
- **Low Friction**: No sign-up required for the initial audit. We prove value before asking for data.
- **CFO-Ready Reports**: Beautiful, shareable results that speak the language of finance, not just engineering.
