## FAQ

**1. How does the audit engine work?**
We use a deterministic engine based on the latest publicly available pricing for 8+ major AI vendors. We compare your seat counts and use cases against plan thresholds to find the "perfect fit" tier for your team.

**2. Is my data secure?**
Yes. Your audit data is processed locally in your browser and is only stored in our database if you choose to "Save & Share" your report. We never share your company details with third parties.

**3. Why do I need this if I only use 2 tools?**
Even with 2 tools, you might be over-provisioned. For example, many small teams pay for "Team" plans when individual "Pro" accounts would provide the same AI capabilities for 20% less cost.

**4. What is Credex?**
Credex is our partner that provides discounted AI infrastructure credits. If your audit shows significant savings opportunities, we connect you with Credex to help you capture that value immediately.

**5. Does this support API usage costs?**
Yes. We have specific optimization rules for OpenAI and Anthropic API spend, suggesting engineering patterns like caching and prompt compression for high-volume users.
