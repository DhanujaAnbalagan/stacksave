# Economics of StackSave — Credex Lead Gen

## 1. Unit Economics of a Lead
We treat every "High Savings" audit (> $500/mo) as a high-intent sales lead for Credex.

- **Average Customer AI Spend**: $2,500/mo ($30,000/yr).
- **Credex Revenue Model**: 10% commission/margin on discounted credits.
- **LTV (Year 1)**: $3,000 per customer.
- **Estimated Conversion (Audit → Consult → Sale)**: 5% (conservative).
- **Lead Value (Audit Completed)**: $3,000 * 0.05 = **$150 per high-savings audit**.

## 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Our GTM plan focuses on organic and community-driven distribution.
- **Engineering Time (Initial Build)**: 40 hours @ $100/hr = $4,000.
- **Maintenance/API Costs**: $20/mo.
- **Amortized CAC (over 1,000 audits)**: $4,020 / 1,000 = **$4.02 per audit**.

**Profitability Ratio**: $150 (Lead Value) / $4.02 (CAC) = **37x ROI**. This is an extremely efficient lead-generation engine.

## 3. The Path to $1M ARR
To drive $1M in ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) through StackSave leads, we need to facilitate ~$10M in credit volume.

**The Math:**
- **Goal Revenue**: $1,000,000.
- **Commission (10%)**: Requires $10M in tool spend managed.
- **Average Spend per Startup**: $30,000/yr.
- **Required Active Customers**: 333 startups.
- **Required Audits (5% conversion)**: 6,660 audits over 18 months.
- **Daily Target**: ~12 audits per day.

## 4. Scaling the Funnel
To hit the $1M ARR target, the following must be true:
1. **Tool Breadth**: We must expand beyond the 8 current tools to include high-spend "AI Adjacent" tools like Pinecone, LangChain Cloud, and Weights & Biases.
2. **Benchmark Mode**: We need to show "Companies like yours spend $X." This FOMO is the strongest driver for "Audit → Consult" conversion.
3. **Automated Monitoring**: Move from a one-time audit to a "Connect your email for monthly bill tracking" model. This increases the surface area for Credex to intervene when spend spikes.

## 5. Summary
StackSave is not a cost center; it is a high-margin customer acquisition engine. By providing $1,000+ of "found money" to a founder, we earn the trust required to manage their entire infrastructure spend.
